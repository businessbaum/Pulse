# Lore concept (working title: "Pulse")

This is a first direction, and every name is a placeholder. Section 8 lists the small part a first version needs.

## 1. Premise

Every machine in the land once moved to one rhythm: the **Pulse**. Then the Pulse was split into five pieces and the land fell still. You are the Rig, the last machine with a living shard of the Pulse in its reactor. You drive out from the Roundhouse to bring the pieces home, and every piece you bring back wakes something that was asleep.

## 2. The world

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **The Pulse** | The power that drove every machine. It is now split into five strands: Heat, Shock, Cryo, Oil and Magnet. |
| **The Still** | The land since the split. Most machines stand where they stopped. |
| **Strays** | Machines that kept moving on splinters of the Pulse. They have no rhythm of their own and are drawn to any living Pulse, which is why they swarm the Rig. |
| **Wardens** | Five great machines. Each holds one strand and has shaped a region around it. |
| **The Roundhouse** | An old engine shed with a turntable and six bays. It is the home base. Its machines are dormant and wake as the Pulse returns. |
| **The Rig** | The player's machine. Its reactor holds a shard of the Pulse. |
| **Beats** | The smallest splinters of the Pulse. Every Stray carries one or two, and a dungeon holds a hoard of them. They all throb in one shared rhythm, on the ground and in the counter. Beats are what a run brings home. |
| **Cores** | Large splinters of the Pulse carried by Wardens. The rarer, heavier currency between runs; not in the prototype yet. |
| **Echoes** | Larger fragments of a strand. Carrying one bends a rule for the rest of the run. |

## 3. Story direction

1. **Wake.** The Rig starts alone in a dark Roundhouse. The first runs bring back Cores and the first machines wake. They remember little, but each knows its trade.
2. **Gather.** Each Warden guards one strand. Beating a Warden frees its region: the Strays there fall quiet and the strand comes home. The Roundhouse fills with light and tools, and the woken machines start to disagree about what happens once the Pulse is whole.
3. **Decide.** With all five strands home, the sixth bay opens. What is inside is the open question of the story.

Both hooks are in here: something to free (the regions and the sleeping machines) and a power to pursue (the Pulse and its fragments).

## 4. Why the mechanics exist

| Mechanic | In the world |
|---|---|
| Reactor | The Rig's shard of the Pulse. Different reactors are different shards. |
| Wires | The paths the Pulse takes through the Rig. |
| States | The five strands. Weapons carry traces of them. |
| Reactions | Two strands meeting on one target. They try to rejoin, and the result is violent. |
| Fusions | Two strands rejoined for good inside one weapon. |
| Hordes | Strays hunt anything with a living Pulse. |
| Death and restart | When the Rig breaks, its shard returns to the Roundhouse and a new body is built. |
| Going home | The shard can pull its body back along the Pulse to the Roundhouse while it is still whole. It needs a few quiet seconds to gather itself, and any blow scatters it. |
| The arms at the turntable | The Roundhouse never stopped building. Its arms work on whatever stands on the deck, whether or not anything needs doing. |
| Frames | Bodies the Smelter casts for the shard. A better body is paid for with Beats, the splinters the Strays carried. |
| Abilities on dropped parts | A part that ran inside a Stray has taken on something of the Pulse that drove it. The stronger the Stray, the more of it the part has kept. Parts the Roundhouse made itself are plain. |
| Levels | The Tuner presses Beats into a part until it holds more than it was built to. |
| Rooms behind the bay doors | The Roundhouse is larger than its hall. Its doors open for what the Strays carried, one at a time. |
| Legacy part | The one part the shard would not let go of. |
| Codex | The Archivist's record of what the strands do together, of the Strays and of the Echoes. Knowing is its trade, so it pays in Beats for everything that is new to it. |
| Veins | Where Beats lie in the ground long enough, they grow together. A blow sets them loose again. |
| Caches | Crates the Roundhouse sent out when its machines still drove, and never got back. They open for a living Pulse that stays with them. The Strays around hear a lock give, and come. |
| Threat levels | A louder Pulse carries farther, and more Strays hear it. |

The same idea repeats at every scale. A reaction rejoins two strands for a moment, a fusion for a run, and the story for good.

## 5. Echoes

Echoes are the fragments of the Pulse that give special effects.

- Whatever held a large piece of the Pulse gives one up when it falls: a dungeon, a Warden, a boss among the Strays.
- They need no slot and cost no Power. A run holds at most three.
- Each one changes a single rule for the rest of the run.
- The shard on the ground is not one Echo but the chance of one: it sounds in three ways at once, and the Rig can only take one of them with it.
- Eight belong to no strand and are there from the first run. The Echo of a strand joins them once the place that held it has been cleared for the first time.

| Echo | Strand | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Second Beat** | None | Every reaction goes off twice; the second is half as strong |
| **Undertow** | None | Drops fly to the Rig from a distance |
| **Crescendo** | None | Every kill adds damage until the Rig loses hull |
| **Afterburn** | None | Nitro refills twice as fast and leaves fire behind |
| **Double Time** | None | Every weapon fires faster |
| **Encore** | None | Once, the blow that would destroy the Rig does not |
| **Heavy Purse** | None | Beats count double, and nothing leaves a heart |
| **Long Wire** | None | Every system reaches every weapon |
| **Ember** | Heat | What dies burning sets its neighbours alight. Held by Kiln. |
| **Afterimage** | Shock | Every tenth hit chains lightning. Held in the Cinder Pit. |
| **Deep Cold** | Cryo | Chill lasts twice as long and slows harder. Held in the Rime Vault. |
| **Seep** | Oil | The Rig's oil lies three times as long. Held in the Tar Sump. |

Not built: **Overtone** (the first fusion of a run needs level 4), which waits for fusions, and **Polarity** (magnetized enemies pull each other together), which waits for Magnet.

Echoes are where the extreme combinations come from. Second Beat on a build that already chains reactions is meant to feel unfair.

## 6. Regions and Wardens

| Strand | Region | Warden | What is special there |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heat | Cinder Flats | Kiln | Ground vents ignite anything oiled |
| Shock | Static Plain | Thunderhead | Lightning strikes, and metal wrecks conduct it |
| Cryo | Frostline | Rime | Icy ground; everything slides |
| Oil | Tar Marsh | Wellhead | Strays arrive already oiled |
| Magnet | Ironfall | Lodestone | Loose scrap flies at magnetized targets |

Each region hands the player one state for free, so a build there only needs the second half of a reaction.

**Kiln** is the first Warden and the only one built so far. It is a walking furnace five metres tall, with anvils for fists and its fire showing through a grate in its chest. Its keep stands in the north-east of the Cinder Flats: black stone, iron on top, chimneys that glow. The arena lies under the keep, and Strays of every region fill the stands around it. They are on Kiln's side: they roar when the Rig is hit, groan when Kiln runs itself into a wall, and sink out of sight when it falls. At half its hull Kiln burns its plating off and comes back as **Meltdown**. What burned inside it comes home as the Kiln Reactor.

## 7. Tone and telling

- Quiet and mechanical, a little melancholy at the start and more hopeful as the Roundhouse fills.
- No cutscenes and no voice acting. The story is told by the Roundhouse changing, by two-line remarks from woken machines, and by Codex entries.
- Nobody in the world is human. Every character is a machine, which keeps art and animation cheap.

## 8. Start small

The first version needs only this:

- The premise as written
- The Roundhouse with three working bays (see lobby.md)
- One region, the Cinder Flats, and one Warden, Kiln
- The three neutral Echoes, plus Ember once Kiln is beaten
- Story step 1 and the first beat of step 2

Everything else in this file is direction for later.
